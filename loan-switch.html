Carry benefit (USD) ≈ Principal × (USD all-in − target all-in) × tenor. FX loss on principal (USD) ≈ Principal × funding-currency appreciation. Net benefit = carry − FX loss. Haircut cost = Principal × mismatch haircut; if it exceeds free equity the switch breaches collateral and additional pledge or a smaller draw is required.
